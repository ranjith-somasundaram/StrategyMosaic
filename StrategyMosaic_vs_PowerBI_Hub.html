Strategy Mosaic vs Microsoft Power BI

Power BI is the default, and the seat looks cheap. Power BI is strong, widely adopted and deeply integrated with Microsoft. The conversation to win sits in the stack underneath it: Fabric capacity, Copilot compute, viewer licensing, and business semantics optimised for one ecosystem. Mosaic's answer is governed semantics as an independent layer, reusable across data platforms, BI tools, applications and AI.

01Power BI at a glance
Core identity
Microsoft's BI & semantic-modelling experience
  • A product in its own right, deeply integrated with Fabric: semantic models, OneLake, Copilot and Fabric IQ.
  • 35M+ monthly active users.
  • Pro is bundled in Microsoft 365 E5, which is why many enterprises treat it as "free".
Analyst position
Gartner Leader · 19th year
  • Microsoft is a Leader in the 2026 Gartner MQ for ABI Platforms for the 19th consecutive year.
  • Strategy is a Visionary in the same MQ. Strategy sees this as aligned with its focus on governed semantics and AI-ready analytics.
AI posture
Copilot + Fabric IQ
  • Copilot needs paid F2+ or P1+ capacity. Trial capacity doesn't qualify. Usage consumes capacity units.
  • Microsoft announced Fabric IQ as generally available at Build 2026, but Microsoft Learn still labels the workload as preview. Ontology remains in preview.
  • Copilot runs on Microsoft-managed Azure OpenAI models. You can't bring your own model to Copilot.
Architectural reality
Capacity-metered, Microsoft-cloud-centric
  • Fabric workloads, Copilot included, consume capacity units from the assigned capacity. Sustained demand can lead to throttling or require scaling.
  • Semantic-model memory limits apply per SKU. F64 allows 25 GB.
  • Analytical measures are expressed in DAX, and models are managed through TMDL.

Copilot is metered compute

Copilot consumes capacity units from the same capacity that runs refreshes and reports. Sustained AI demand becomes a capacity-planning question, potentially meaning throttling or a larger SKU.

Free-viewer threshold: F64+

On F2 through F32, internal report viewers still need Pro. The exception is App-Owns-Data embedding for external users. Large internal audiences either license every viewer or move to F64.

MEA data boundary

Per Microsoft Learn, where the tenant or capacity is outside the US or EU data boundary, the cross-geography processing setting Copilot relies on is disabled by default and may need tenant-admin enablement. That is a residency conversation for regulated GCC accounts.

Memory caps by SKU

Semantic-model memory limits per SKU: F32 allows 10 GB, F64 25 GB, F128 50 GB. For import models, memory becomes a sizing factor alongside user count. Model optimisation or other storage modes are the alternatives.

Reserve or pause, not both

Reservations lower the rate but bill continuously. Pay-as-you-go can pause, but at a higher hourly rate. Either way, BI now needs capacity monitoring and FinOps discipline.

The line items keep coming

OneLake storage, separate M365 Copilot SKUs, Purview/compliance licensing and E5 renewals are each priced separately from Power BI itself.

Q&A retires in February 2027. Copilot is the successor, and Copilot requires capacity.

Microsoft is retiring Power BI Q&A, which was included with Pro. Existing Q&A visuals in reports, dashboards, mobile and embedded will show an error. Microsoft recommends Copilot as the successor, and Copilot needs qualifying paid Fabric or Premium capacity. Microsoft states it currently has no plans for a Copilot replacement for embedded (PaaS) Q&A. Pro-only customers using natural-language questions today face a capacity decision.

04Your region, your cloud, your data centre

This is the play for regulated, sovereign and public-sector accounts across MEA. Power BI's full capability, Copilot and Fabric included, is delivered as Microsoft cloud SaaS. Strategy can run where the customer decides: in Strategy's managed cloud, in the customer's own cloud account, or on Kubernetes inside the customer's own data centre.

Power BI deployment model

Microsoft cloud SaaS, with a narrower on-prem option

  • The Power BI service, Fabric, Copilot and Fabric IQ are Microsoft-operated SaaS in Microsoft's cloud.
  • Power BI Report Server is the on-prem route. It supports a subset of Power BI features, and Copilot and Fabric workloads are service-only.
  • Outside the US/EU boundary, Copilot's cross-geo processing setting is off by default and may need admin enablement.
  • Capacity, updates and the operating environment are Microsoft's to run.
Customer Managed Cloud · on-prem
Your own data centre

CMC deploys the full Strategy platform on the customer's own on-prem Kubernetes (Red Hat-certified), under the customer's ingress and egress rules. Automation runs locally, without connectivity back to external services.

Customer Managed Cloud · public cloud
Your own cloud account

Runs in the customer's own AWS, Azure or GCP account, in the region they choose. The customer controls system and network isolation, and usage can count toward existing cloud commitments.

Strategy-managed
Managed Cloud Enterprise

Fully managed by Strategy on AWS, Azure, GCP or STACKIT, with multi-environment high availability and a dedicated customer success team.

Model endpoint choice
Your AI, your boundary

BYOE model endpoints, plus open-source and offline model options. The customer decides where AI runs, alongside where the platform runs.

05How Strategy wins · 6 differentiators

We don't compete on Microsoft ecosystem fit. We compete on what the buyer inherits after year one: business semantics independent of any single ecosystem, choice in AI models and deployment, observability across the tools that consume those semantics, and long-standing depth in governed enterprise reporting and distribution.

01 / Semantic independence
One open, portable definition vs DAX inside a Fabric workspace
Power BI
  • Measures are expressed in DAX and managed in Fabric/Power BI workspaces.
  • Best consumed inside Microsoft: Power BI, Excel, Copilot.
  • Moving away can mean translating DAX, metadata and model semantics into the target platform.
Strategy
  • Mosaic is a Universal Semantic Layer over 200+ sources, with no forced move to OneLake.
  • Mosaic imports and exports Apache Ossie (Incubating, formerly OSI) YAML. Models can move across Ossie-compatible tools, subject to documented conversion limitations.
  • Security policies are centralised and enforced across supported consumption paths.
The ask that lands
"If you add a second BI tool, a Databricks agent, or a new cloud next year, where does your 'Net Revenue' definition live, and who rewrites it?"
02 / Every tool, one truth
Reusable across Power BI, Tableau, Excel and agents vs optimised for Microsoft experiences
Power BI
  • Semantic models are optimised for Power BI and Microsoft consumers.
  • Interoperability exists via XMLA and APIs, but cross-BI semantic serving isn't the primary design goal.
Strategy
  • One governed Mosaic model can serve Power BI, Tableau, Excel, custom apps and Strategy Agents.
  • It can sit underneath Power BI. Users keep their reports and the definitions become governed.
The ask that lands
"How many versions of 'Active Customer' exist across your Power BI workspaces, Excel files and other tools today, and which one would your CFO sign?"
03 / AI freedom
Model choice and open agent access vs Microsoft-managed Copilot models
Power BI
  • Copilot runs on Microsoft-managed Azure OpenAI models, with no bring-your-own model for Copilot.
  • Power BI and Fabric IQ MCP servers are in preview, and require tenant-admin enablement.
  • Legacy Q&A retires Feb 2027. Copilot, which needs capacity, is the successor.
Strategy
  • Azure OpenAI, BYOE Gemini, and open-source/offline models.
  • MCP integration connects governed agents and Mosaic models to compatible AI clients. Mosaic MCP is in preview.
  • AI is grounded on certified objects, and AI Sync re-validates it when models change.
The ask that lands
"If your group standardises on a different model provider next year, can your BI copilot follow, or does it stay on Microsoft's choice?"
04 / Sovereignty & deployment
Your cloud, your region, your model endpoint vs Microsoft cloud SaaS
Power BI
  • Microsoft cloud SaaS. The on-prem Report Server has a narrower feature set.
  • Outside the US/EU boundary, Copilot's cross-geo processing setting is off by default and may need admin enablement.
Strategy
  • Managed cloud, or the customer's own AWS, Azure, GCP or STACKIT tenancy, or on-prem.
  • Choose the model endpoint and region. Offline models are available for strict residency.
The ask that lands
"Where the AI service isn't available locally, which cross-geo processing settings would need enabling, and what does that mean for your residency requirements?"
05 / Governance you can see
Runtime visibility across the tools consuming your semantics, Power BI included
Power BI
  • Admin monitoring, Capacity Metrics and Purview cover the Microsoft estate.
  • Purview catalogues broadly, but runtime semantic-consumption visibility centres on Microsoft workloads.
Strategy
  • Mosaic Sentinel covers Risk, Audit & Compliance, Usage, Cost Intelligence and Query Performance.
  • The Sep 2026 release captures query performance for Mosaic access from Power BI, Tableau, Strategy Agents and MCP clients.
The ask that lands
"Show me one view of who hit your Revenue metric last month, from a dashboard, from Tableau and from an AI agent, with how long each query took."
06 / Enterprise delivery at scale
Different heritage: broad self-service vs deep governed enterprise distribution
Power BI
  • Excellent, broadly adopted self-service BI. Paginated reports are a separate report type.
  • At scale, capacity sizing (memory limits, refresh load, concurrency) becomes an ongoing task.
Strategy
  • Pixel-perfect reporting, bursting and multi-schedule subscriptions, plus native mobile apps.
  • HyperIntelligence surfaces governed insight cards in supported web apps and email, without custom integration.
  • Test Center regression-tests SQL, data, PDF and performance before changes go live.
The ask that lands
"How do you deliver 5,000 personalised statements every month, and prove nothing changed after the last upgrade?"
